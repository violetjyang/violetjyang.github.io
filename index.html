I am an incoming Clinical Psychology PhD student at Southern Methodist University. My research integrates computational and quantitative methods with clinical science to advance understanding of psychopathology. I am particularly interested in using dynamic systems theory, intensive longitudinal methods, and generative AI to study the architecture of mental health conditions and quantify internal phenomenology.
